01 · APPLICATION CONTEXT

Supporting the full commercial policy lifecycle

An enterprise platform used by insurance agents across the lifecycle of a commercial insurance policy.

The application supports the end-to-end commercial policy lifecycle, covering quote and submission through underwriting, binding, policy issuance, endorsements and renewal.

Insurance agents are the primary users of the platform, managing policy-related activities across these different stages within a single application.

03 · FEATURE FOCUS

Vymo integration

Connecting an external platform to bring lead information into the agent-facing application.

A key feature I am currently working on is the integration between the commercial lines application and Vymo through APIs.

Vymo returns lead information that can be brought into the policy administration experience for agents. The integration requires the external service to work within the application's existing logic, data structure and user workflow.

This work combines API integration with application development — connecting the external service and handling the returned information within an established enterprise platform.
