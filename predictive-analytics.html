The objective was to use historical user data to estimate conversion likelihood and understand the factors associated with users dropping out before conversion.

Rather than using the model only as a prediction mechanism, the analysis also focused on feature importance — identifying which variables were most strongly associated with conversion and drop-off behavior so that the results could inform engagement and user experience decisions.
